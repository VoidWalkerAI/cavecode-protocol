# CaveCode Scaffold Contract

`cavecode_new_card.py` prints one of two v1.0 profiles:

- `artifact` — a bounded Artifact Map
- `project` — a repository Master Project Map with current-state and handoff
  sections

The scaffold is intentionally a starting point, not a fixed block law. Blocks
may be added, removed, reordered, or reuse glyphs as the artifact requires.

The canonical glyphs remain 🪨, 🖍️, 🔧, 🎮, and 🌐.
