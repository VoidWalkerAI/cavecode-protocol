🪨 BLOCK 1 — PILOT IDENTITY AND PURPOSE
============================================================

RangeWater is the first live CaveCode Forgekeeper pilot.

The pilot tested whether repository-side CaveCode continuity could preserve
current project truth, authority, evidence lineage, handoff state, and history
through a real Android release and Google Play production publication.

This record closes the pilot. It does not claim that a separate Forgekeeper
engine exists.

============================================================
🎮 BLOCK 4 — RESULTS
============================================================

PASS — AUTHORITY PRESERVATION

The automation did not ratify project truth. Michael remained final authority.

PASS — EVIDENCE LINEAGE

The repository records connect source, tests, physical acceptance, Play
migration, production authority, publication, and later supersession.

PASS — HISTORY PRESERVATION

Historical captures and the legacy master ledger were preserved rather than
silently rewritten or deleted.

PASS — COLD-START RECOVERY

A new human or AI can begin with the RangeWater continuity contract and active
current-state index and recover the current release state.

PASS — CONFLICT AND DRIFT RECOVERY

The pilot detected branch drift and stale current-state language and recorded
explicit resolutions.

QUALIFIED FAIL — CC-009 SAME-COMMIT SYNCHRONIZATION

Meaningful product-state changes and the master-map repair did not remain atomic
throughout the release sequence. The final repository state is explicit and
auditable, but later continuity commits repaired earlier drift.

NOT TESTED — AUTOMATED FORGEKEEPER ENGINE

The pilot workflow was performed through human direction and AI-assisted
repository operations. No standalone Forgekeeper engine ran this pilot.


============================================================
🔧 BLOCK 5 — FRICTION DISCOVERED
============================================================

FRICTION 1 — LARGE LEGACY MASTER MAP

The RangeWater root master ledger contained broad historical and sensitive
operational material. The active connector would not safely republish the whole
file merely to replace stale current-state lines.

FRICTION 2 — DISTRIBUTED CURRENT TRUTH

Current truth was spread across the root master ledger, a short current-state
index, workorders, captures, transition records, and Git history.

FRICTION 3 — POST-EVENT RECONCILIATION

Fast external events such as Google Play approval and publication were recorded
after they occurred. This created a period in which repository entry points did
not agree.

FRICTION 4 — GOVERNANCE OVERLAY

RangeWater's continuity ladder is more detailed than core CaveCode v1.0.
An engine must load project-specific governance without treating it as a silent
change to the universal protocol.


============================================================
🪨 BLOCK 6 — REQUIRED ENGINE LESSONS
============================================================

A Forgekeeper engine must:

- inspect before proposing changes;
- identify the authoritative repository and exact ref;
- distinguish the universal CaveCode contract from project-specific governance;
- default to read-only operation;
- detect competing current-state claims;
- refuse to infer human ratification;
- produce CAPTURED evidence and PROPOSED repairs with exact source pointers;
- prefer atomic state-map updates with meaningful implementation changes;
- mark continuity incomplete when atomic synchronization cannot occur;
- support explicit append-only supersession when safe direct repair is blocked;
- avoid exposing secrets or republishing sensitive operational material;
- preserve raw evidence separately from normalized interpretations;
- leave an auditable cold-start resume point.


============================================================
🌐 BLOCK 7 — PUBLIC-SAFE FINDING
============================================================

RangeWater completed the first live CaveCode Forgekeeper pilot during a real
Google Play production release. The pilot validated repository-side continuity,
authority preservation, evidence lineage, recovery, and supersession, while
exposing a same-commit synchronization problem for the future engine to solve.
